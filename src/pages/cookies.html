Cookie Policy

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies, which is why you didn't see a cookie banner. Last updated November 6, 2026.

Cookies for Visitors

None from us. You can browse every page and send us a request without this website setting a single cookie. The Google map is the one outside service that may set its own (see Maps and 3D Tours below). Our visitor statistics work without cookies; the privacy policy explains how.

Cookies for Our Office

These are only set for people who work here. You won't get them by visiting the site.

NameWhat It DoesHow Long
jbh_adminKeeps a staff member signed in to the website's admin area. Only sent to pages under /admin.30 days
jbh_staffSet only on a staff member's device when they sign in to the admin. It opens the admin when they go to the home page, keeps their own visits out of the statistics, and stops someone guessing passwords elsewhere from locking them out.1 year

What Your Browser Keeps for You

If you start filling in a request and leave before sending it, your answers are saved in your browser's storage so they're still there when you come back. They stay on your device and aren't sent to us until you press Send. They're deleted when you send the request, when you tap Start over, or after 14 days. Photos are not saved this way.

If you tap Not now on the note that offers to add us to your home screen, your browser remembers that for 60 days so it doesn't ask again. Your browser also keeps one small page with our phone number and email, so that if you open the site with no connection you can still reach us.

Maps and 3D Tours

The Google map near the bottom of our home page and on our contact page loads from Google when you scroll to it. The Matterport 3D tours on our floor plans page load only when you tap one. Once loaded, Google or Matterport may set their own cookies. Their policies explain what they use: Google, Matterport.

Removing Cookies

Your browser's settings let you see and delete cookies and stored site data at any time. Clearing them won't change how this website works for you.

Selling or Sharing Your Information

We don't sell or share personal information and don't use cookies for advertising. If you have questions, email service@johnnybhome.com.
