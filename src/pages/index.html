Why Build With Us?

For over five decades, Johnny B Home Construction has proudly built homes in the Green Bay area. With BBB accreditation since 1995, we maintain strong relationships with subcontractors, ensuring fairness and quality. With a legacy dating back to 1986, Johnny B Home Construction has seen many changes in home styles, but our three core values have remained unwavering:

We Support Local Business

We have strong relationships with local subcontractors and suppliers, or as we like to call them “Preferred Vendors”. By working with local businesses we can guarantee you the best prices via reduced shipping expenses, all while giving back to the community. The dedication and expertise of our partnered subcontractors is why our homes have such outstanding quality.

Meet Our Preferred Vendors

Unyielding Quality

Our commitment to exceptional craftsmanship, premier materials, and guaranteed satisfaction repair is the foundation of every home we build. From foundation to finishes, our attention to detail ensures a home that exceeds expectations.
