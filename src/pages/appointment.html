    How Soon Will I Hear Back?

    We’ll get back to you as soon as possible. The office is open Monday to Thursday, 9 am to 4 pm, and Friday, 9 am to noon.

    We build homes in the Green Bay area.

    Do You Help With Financing?

    We partner with the Bank of Luxemburg, an equal housing lender in Luxemburg, Wisconsin, for financing throughout the home construction process.
